About EduGate

Simple result access for schools, teachers, and students.

EduGate helps high schools manage student results, user accounts, and academic records through a clear role-based interface.

What EduGate Does

The platform gives students a direct way to view results, gives teachers tools to add and manage scores, and gives administrators control over users and school records.

Students

View results, profile information, and academic progress.

Teachers

Add results and manage student performance records.

Admins

Manage users, schools, and access across the system.
